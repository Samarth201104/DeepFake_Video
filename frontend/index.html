From video to a model signal.

A transparent look at what happens during an analysis.

02
⌗

Frame & face sampling

The backend samples every 10th frame and uses RetinaFace to find faces.

03
◈

Xception inference

Face crops are resized to 256 × 256, normalized, and scored by the trained model.

04
⌁

Face scores are averaged. A mean score at or above 0.5 maps to Fake.

ABOUT FRAMECheck

Research tooling for a more careful look.

This project combines a RetinaFace face detector with a binary Xception classifier trained for real and fake face crops. It is a research/demo tool and can miss manipulated content or flag authentic footage. A score is not proof of authenticity.
